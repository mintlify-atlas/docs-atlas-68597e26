IntelliPlan is an AI-powered student planner built by a student, for students. It connects to Canvas, Google Classroom, StudentVue, Schoology, Blackboard, and Moodle to pull in your assignments automatically, then uses AI to generate a personalized weekly study schedule, export it to Google Calendar, and help you actually understand the material through an adaptive tutor named Plani.

## What IntelliPlan Does

IntelliPlan solves the gap between knowing what is due and knowing when and how to do it. School platforms show you deadlines — IntelliPlan turns them into an actionable plan.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your school platform">
    Link Canvas, Google Classroom, StudentVue, Schoology, Blackboard, or Moodle. Assignments auto-import within seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get a personalized study schedule">
    Tell IntelliPlan your available hours and preferred study time. The AI generates a complete multi-day plan with focused blocks and breaks, then syncs it to Google Calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Study smarter with Plani">
    Upload notes to generate flashcards and quizzes, or ask Plani — the adaptive AI tutor — to teach you any subject step by step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track grades and predict outcomes">
    View your live GPA from connected platforms. Use the Grade Modeler to simulate how your next test score will affect your course grade.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Supported Platforms

IntelliPlan works with the most widely used school platforms:

| Platform | Connection Method |
|---|---|
| **Canvas LMS** | OAuth 2.0 with Developer Key |
| **Google Classroom** | Google OAuth 2.0 |
| **StudentVue** | District URL + credentials |
| **Schoology** | API Key + Secret |
| **Blackboard Learn** | OAuth 2.0 (3LO) |
| **Moodle** | Site URL + Web Services Token |

<Note>
  You can connect multiple platforms simultaneously. IntelliPlan merges assignments from all sources into a single unified view.
</Note>
